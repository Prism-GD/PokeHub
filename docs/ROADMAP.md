## Legal and safety boundary

PokéHub is a catalog and distribution platform for homebrew, original projects, tools, and legally shareable patches. It must not host or distribute commercial copyrighted Pokémon ROM images. ROM hacks should be distributed as patches when possible, with users supplying their own legally obtained game files.
